# Reasoning-First Research Workflow

## Pipeline

User question → research brief → concept graph → search plan → source acquisition → evidence matrix → reasoning chains → contradiction audit → synthesis → answer.

## Evidence grading

- A: primary source, reproducible data, formal proof, official specification, or direct measurement.
- B: peer-reviewed review, high-quality textbook, official analysis, reputable expert synthesis.
- C: credible secondary reporting or practitioner writeup.
- D: unsupported claim, anonymous source, marketing copy, or model-generated claim without verification.

## Chain schema

Each reasoning chain should contain:
- chain_id
- endpoint_claim
- prerequisites
- assumptions
- derivation_steps
- evidence_refs
- confidence
- falsification_tests
- open_questions
