# Stakeholder 1-Pager Template

One reader, one ask, one page. Loaded when the deliverable is an exec brief.
Frame everything for the archetype (`references/stakeholder-archetypes.md`).

```markdown
# {Initiative name} — {one-line outcome}
**For:** {reader name, title}   **Ask:** {the single decision you need}   **Date:** {date}

## The situation today
{Benchmark Zero in the reader's frame — 2–3 sentences. Current cost, who's
affected. Figures labeled [SOURCED] or [GENERATED].}

## What we propose
{The initiative in 2–3 sentences. Outcome, not tooling.}

## Why now
{Time Criticality — what delay costs.}

## What success looks like
{The measurable outcome: metric, baseline → target, window.}

## What we need from you
{The one ask. Be specific: approve pilot / fund Phase 1 / unlock domain.}

## Phase 1 at a glance
{Goal, entry gate, kill criterion, duration — one line each.}

---
Open items: {[FILL: …] list, or "none"}
```

## Rules

- Fits on one page. If it doesn't, cut detail, not the ask.
- Lead with the reader's metric (see archetypes).
- Never present an unverified number as fact — label or `[FILL: …]`.
