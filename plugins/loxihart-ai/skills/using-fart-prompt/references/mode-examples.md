# Mode Examples — Fart Prompt

Concrete before/after examples per mode. Each shows a "stuck" input and
what the fart output should look like vs. what to avoid.

---

## WRITING MODE

**Stuck input:** "I need to write the opening of a memoir chapter about my first job."

**BAD fart output (too polished):**
> "My first job taught me many things about responsibility and hard work. Looking back, I can see how those early experiences shaped who I am today."

**GOOD fart output:**
> "I showed up twenty minutes late on day one. Parked in the wrong spot. Wore the wrong thing, I think? There were so many people and I kept nodding like I understood the org chart. My manager's name was either [PERSON] or Dan. The coffee tasted like burnt plastic and I drank three cups anyway because I needed something to do with my hands."

---

## PLANNING MODE

**Stuck input:** "I need to plan Q3 FlashFusion content."

**BAD fart output (too structured):**
> "Here's a strategic content plan for Q3: 1. Establish themes. 2. Map to audience segments. 3. Build a content calendar."

**GOOD fart output:**
> "Write about burnout again probably. The AI tools roundup nobody reads but gets good SEO. Something about n8n. Maybe do a hot take that gets engagement but I regret it. Workshop promo — but when? The Workflow of the Week backlog is a mess. Need to actually define the audience first but keep avoiding that. A collab with someone? Who? Reply to the three people who emailed. The newsletter is three weeks behind."

---

## IDEAS MODE

**Stuck input:** "Newsletter angles about AI burnout for FlashFusion readers."

**BAD fart output:**
> "Here are 5 thoughtful angles on AI burnout: the productivity paradox, tool fatigue, the always-on problem..."

**GOOD fart output:**
> "1. AI made me work harder, not less (too obvious) / 2. The actual problem is you have 40 tools and none of them talk to each other / 3. Unplugging from AI for a week: I became useless / 4. Your AI stack is a substitute for a decision / 5. The tool you're using is designed to keep you using the tool / 6. Burnout is a skill issue, actually (controversial, probably wrong) / 7. What if the output is fine and the anxiety is the problem / 8. I automated the thing I actually needed to do myself"

---

## PERSONAL MODE

**Stuck input:** "I can't decide whether to push the Loxihart runbook forward or focus on FlashFusion this week."

**BAD fart output:**
> "Let me help you think through this decision systematically. Consider your priorities, timelines, and stakeholder expectations..."

**GOOD fart output:**
> "You're going to do FlashFusion but feel guilty about Loxihart. Or you'll do Loxihart and resent it. The runbook isn't going anywhere. FlashFusion is the thing you actually want to work on. You already know the answer. What's actually stopping you?"
