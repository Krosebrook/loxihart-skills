# Script Structure — Full Arc

Every HeyGen script follows this five-beat arc. Beats are internal structure labels — do NOT write them into the script output.

---

## The Beats

### HOOK — 10–15% of total

One sentence that earns the next 60 seconds.

**Rules:**
- Statement, not question.
- Creates tension, surprise, or a claim that demands resolution.
- No credential-leading openers ("As a former hospitality manager..." → that belongs in the bridge).
- The listener should think "how?" or "what does that mean?" — not "I've heard this."

**Strong hook patterns:**
- Contradiction: "Ten years in hotels taught me more about AI than any certification."
- Gap statement: "Most companies are automating the wrong things."
- Specific claim: "The gap between what AI can do and what most people think it can do is where I live."

**Weak hook patterns (avoid):**
- Credential opener: "As someone who's worked in both hospitality and AI..."
- Generic insight: "AI is changing how we work."
- Rhetorical question: "Ever wonder why your competitors seem to do more with less?"

---

### CREDIBILITY BRIDGE — 10–15%

Why Kyle is speaking on this. Earned, not claimed.

**Pattern:** What Kyle did → what it taught him → what that unlocks now.

The standard arc: hospitality management (people, operations, pressure) → self-taught AI (no CS background, built from scratch) → what that combination makes possible for the audience.

**When to skip this beat:**
- Client-facing demos where Kyle is not the subject
- Short-form clips (60s) where word count is too tight — compress to one sentence or cut entirely
- If skipping: expand THE WORK by 10–15% to fill

---

### THE GAP — 20–25%

The problem or distinction the audience hasn't named yet.

**Pattern:** "Most people X. The difference is Y."

- Should create mild discomfort of recognition — the audience knows this problem but hasn't articulated it
- Avoid generic pain points: "businesses struggle with efficiency" is not a gap, it's a category
- The gap should be specific enough that the audience thinks "that's exactly it"

**Strong gap examples:**
- "Most teams treat AI as a productivity tool. The ones winning treat it as a reasoning partner."
- "People want AI to do their job for them. What it's actually good at is doing the parts of your job that don't require judgment."

---

### THE WORK — 35–40%

What Kyle actually does. Specific. No category fluff.

- Name the audience explicitly: domain expert, curious learner, or both
- If tutorial: the core instruction lives here — concrete steps, not abstractions
- If case study: specific outcome with context, not generic benefit ("reduced review time by eliminating one full approval round" not "saved time")
- If brand intro: the concrete offer — what Kyle delivers, to whom, in what format

---

### SOFT CLOSE — 10–15%

No hard CTA. One sentence invitation.

- Platform-agnostic unless user specifies destination
- Default: "Find me at FlashFusion.co."
- Do not close with urgency language ("before it's too late", "limited spots")
- Do not close with desperation ("I'd love to connect", "don't hesitate to reach out")

CTA options by destination → see `references/production-prompt.md`

## Sentence Targets by Duration

Use these as calibration guides, not hard rules. A sentence with a Voice Director tag break counts toward pacing rhythm.

| Beat | 60s | 90s | 2 min | 3 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 1 | 1–2 | 2 | 2–3 |
| BRIDGE | 1–2 | 2–3 | 3–4 | 3–5 |
| GAP | 2 | 3–4 | 4–5 | 5–7 |
| WORK | 3–4 | 5–7 | 8–10 | 12–16 |
| CLOSE | 1 | 1–2 | 1–2 | 2 |

## Common Structural Errors

**Error:** Opening with a credential ("I've spent ten years in hospitality and I'm here to tell you...")
→ Fix: Move credential to the bridge beat. Hook with a claim instead.

**Error:** The GAP beat restates the HOOK
→ Fix: Hook = surprising claim. Gap = the underlying reason or mechanism behind it. They should be different.

**Error:** THE WORK beat lists services rather than showing the work
→ Fix: Replace category names ("I do AI consulting") with outcome evidence ("I helped a 4-person ops team cut their weekly reporting from 6 hours to 45 minutes without changing their tools").

**Error:** Closing with multiple CTAs
→ Fix: One destination. If multi-platform, default to "Find me at FlashFusion.co."
