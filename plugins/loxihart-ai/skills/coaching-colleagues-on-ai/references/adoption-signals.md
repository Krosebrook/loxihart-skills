# Adoption Signals — Logging Coaching Outcomes

The survey measures adoption; coaching sessions explain it. Log every session —
this is the qualitative layer the numbers miss.

## Entry format (one block per session, append-only)

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — <first name>, <role/team>
- Persona: beginner | skeptic | power-user
- They tried: <task attempted, tool used>
- Blocker class: access | trust | prompt-skill | data-sensitivity | time | tool-fit
- Outcome: win-shipped | partial | stuck | declined
- Using AI now: yes | sometimes | no
- Follow-up: <one concrete thing, or "none">
```

## Rules

- First names and roles only — no performance judgments, no verbatim quotes
  that could embarrass anyone. This log must be safe to show its subjects.
- No client data or ticket contents in the log — task *types*, not specifics.
- Log declines and failures honestly. "3 people stuck on access requests"
  is exactly the finding leadership needs.

## Monthly rollup (feeds the leadership brief)

Count by blocker class and outcome. Report the top blocker with its count,
one win story (with the person's ok), and the trend vs last month. This
rollup is a primary input to adoption measurement and the enablement
business case — signal quality here compounds.
