# Pipeline Stages & ICP — FlashFusion

## ICP (expands the SKILL.md quick filter)

**Company:** IT services / MSPs / tech-adjacent businesses, 10–200 employees —
big enough to feel ops pain, small enough to lack an internal AI function.
**Decision maker:** IT lead, operations manager, founder, VP of Operations —
whoever owns the friction, not procurement.
**Signals (any strengthens fit):** posted about AI adoption recently · hiring
for AI/automation roles · visible modern-tooling stack · founder/ops lead
personally active online · service-delivery scaling pain in public posts.
**Disqualifiers:** enterprise procurement cycles (>200 emp) · pure body-shop
staffing model · no digital service delivery · active in-house AI team
(they're a peer, not a prospect).

**Deal-size bands / quarterly targets:** [Kyle fills — pull from
managing-ff-finances actuals; no invented figures in this file.]

## Stages (HubSpot is the record of truth)

| Stage | Entry criterion | Exit forward |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect | Matches ICP, source logged | Enriched |
| Enriched | Clay/Apollo data complete: contact, role, signal | Contacted |
| Contacted | First personalized touch sent (Apollo sequence or manual) | Engaged |
| Engaged | *They* responded substantively (auto-reply ≠ engaged) | Discovery |
| Discovery | Call held; pain + budget-authority understood | Proposal |
| Proposal | Scoped offer delivered (route: managing-ff-contracts) | Won / Lost |
| Won | Signed → handoff to contracts + delivery | — |
| Lost | Reason logged (no-budget / timing / no-fit / ghosted) | Recycle in 90d if timing |

## Tool responsibilities (no overlap, no gaps)

- **Apollo** — prospect discovery + sequencing; owns *outreach* state
- **Clay** — enrichment only; writes into HubSpot, holds nothing canonical
- **HubSpot** — canonical record: stage, notes, next action, close reasons

## Hygiene rules

Every open deal past Contacted carries a **next action + date** — a deal
without one is stalled by definition. Weekly pass: anything untouched 14+
days gets advanced, recycled, or closed-lost honestly. Pipeline value ≠
revenue — recognized revenue lives in managing-ff-finances, and the two are
never summed (standing lane rule).
