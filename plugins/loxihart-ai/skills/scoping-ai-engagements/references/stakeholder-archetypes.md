# Stakeholder Archetypes

Framing guide. Loaded when configuring who the brief is for. One reader per
brief — pick the archetype, frame everything in their language.

| Archetype | Frame AI as | They care about | They distrust |
|---|---|---|---|
| President / CEO | Revenue enablement, competitive risk, cost delta | Top/bottom line, market position | Tech detail, hype without numbers |
| VP Operations | Hours saved, error reduction, scalable process | Throughput, reliability, headcount leverage | Anything that adds process friction |
| IT / Service Desk Manager | Faster triage, KB quality, ticket deflection | MTTR, deflection rate, team load | Tools that create more tickets |
| Engineering lead | Toil reduction, build velocity, skill growth | Developer time, maintainability | Black boxes, vendor lock-in |

## Using the frame

- **Open with their metric.** CEO → cost/revenue delta; VP Ops → hours/week; IT → deflection %; Eng → toil hours.
- **One ask per brief.** Approve the pilot, fund Phase 1, unlock a domain — pick one.
- **Match proof to skepticism.** Give the CEO numbers, give the Eng lead architecture, give Ops a process-impact note.

## Calibration note

Regulated or sensitive-data environments lower feasibility and raise the
compliance framing for every archetype — surface it early rather than burying it.
