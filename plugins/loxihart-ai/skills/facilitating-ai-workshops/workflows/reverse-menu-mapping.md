# Workflow: Reverse Menu Mapping

<required_reading>
Read `references/exercises.md` — Section 1 (Reverse Menu Mapping) before proceeding.
</required_reading>

<process>
## Step 1: Frame the Exercise

Tell the group the single rule before anything else:
> "No one names an AI tool until columns 1 and 2 of the Outcome Ledger are fully populated."

If anyone jumps to column 3 early, redirect: "What number changes if this works?"

## Step 2: Distribute the Outcome Ledger

Present the three-column table. Participants work in teams of 2–4.

| TARGET METRIC CHANGE | HUMAN FRICTION POINT | AI ENABLER |
|---|---|---|
| Before → After (specific number) | What step causes the gap? | Name this last |

## Step 3: Facilitate Column 1

Push for specificity. "Improve efficiency" fails. "Reduce first-response time from 4 hours to 15 minutes" passes.

**Red flags:**
- Vague metric → ask "what number changes if this works?"
- Department named instead of a step → ask "what does a person physically do?"
- Column 3 filled first → redirect, do not proceed

## Step 4: Facilitate Column 2

The friction point is a human action, not a system or team. "Aggregating data from three dashboards manually every Friday" is a friction point. "The legal team" is not.

## Step 5: Unlock Column 3

Only after columns 1 and 2 are complete. Teams select the AI enabler that directly addresses column 2 to achieve column 1.

## Step 6: Stack-Rank the Backlog

Prioritize by: (business impact of metric change) × (feasibility of AI enabler). Top 3 items become the AI Project Backlog output.

## Step 7: Deliver the Artifact

Output: Prioritized AI Project Backlog — a table of completed Outcome Ledger rows, ranked. If chaining to Exercise 2, pass the top items forward.
</process>

<success_criteria>
Exercise 1 is complete when:
- [ ] Every row has a before/after number in column 1
- [ ] Every row has a specific human action (not a department) in column 2
- [ ] No column 3 was filled before columns 1 and 2 were locked
- [ ] Backlog is stack-ranked by impact
- [ ] Artifact delivered: Prioritized AI Project Backlog
</success_criteria>
