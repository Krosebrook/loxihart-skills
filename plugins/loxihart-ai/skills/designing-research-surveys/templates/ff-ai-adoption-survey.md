# Template — AI Adoption Baseline Survey

**Use:** before any enablement initiative, re-run at 60–90 days. The delta is
the evidence. Anonymous; SurveyMonkey link via email or Teams (never Kixie or
ticket). ~3 minutes, 9 questions.

## Questions

1. How often do you use AI tools for work tasks?
   ○ Daily ○ Few times a week ○ Few times a month ○ Tried once or twice ○ Never
2. Which have you used for work in the last 30 days? *(select all)*
   ☐ Claude ☐ Copilot ☐ ChatGPT ☐ Gemini ☐ Other: ___ ☐ None
3. What tasks have you used AI for? *(select all)*
   ☐ Drafting emails/docs ☐ Summarizing ☐ Troubleshooting/lookup
   ☐ Data/spreadsheets ☐ Code/scripts ☐ Brainstorming ☐ Other: ___ ☐ Haven't used it
4. Confidence getting a *useful* result when you try: 1–5
   *(1 = rarely useful · 5 = almost always)*
5. Biggest blocker to using AI more: *(pick one)*
   ○ Don't know where to start ○ Don't trust the output ○ No access/licensing
   ○ Not allowed for my data ○ No time to learn ○ Doesn't fit my tasks ○ No blocker
6. In a typical week, AI saves me roughly:
   ○ None ○ <30 min ○ 30–60 min ○ 1–3 hrs ○ 3+ hrs
   *(self-report band — report it as such, never as a measured figure)*
7. Have you checked an AI answer and found it wrong in the last month?
   ○ Yes, caught it ○ Yes, it slipped through ○ No ○ Haven't used it enough
8. What would help you most next? *(pick one)*
   ○ 1:1 walkthrough on my real task ○ Short team demo ○ Written how-tos
   ○ Approved-use clarity ○ Better access ○ Nothing needed
9. One task you wish AI could take off your plate: *(open text)*

## Analysis keys

Adoption = Q1 top-2 boxes. Blocker distribution (Q5) drives the enablement
plan. Q7 "slipped through" count is the QA/risk signal. Q9 open text feeds
the use-case backlog verbatim. Report deltas only against the same anonymous
cohort framing — never attribute individual rows.
