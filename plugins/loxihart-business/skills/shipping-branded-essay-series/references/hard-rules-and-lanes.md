## Lane map

- **Personal / Abstrekkt (this skill):** Load-Bearing, FlashFusion, Abstrekkt
  content. Self-initiated. This is where the series lives.
- **Day job / LOXIHART:** absolute separation. Never referenced here in any framing.
  Loxihart content has its own skills.
- **Memoir ("Tax the Damage" / "Construction, Not Recovery"):** protected,
  separate voice register. Never cross-pollinate into Load-Bearing.

## Provenance discipline

- `[SOURCED]` requires a real, openable citation — a doc, a measurement, a shipped
  file. If it can't be opened, it is not `[SOURCED]`.
- `[GENERATED]` must show the reasoning, not just assert.
- No invented precision. "I cannot verify this" beats a confident guess.

## The day-job gate (why anonymization does not save it)

If a post describes a real incident at the employer, redacting the name does not
make it safe — the lane separation is on the *event*, not the label. Gate it,
confirm the context (day-job vs FlashFusion/client), and cut if day-job-adjacent.
