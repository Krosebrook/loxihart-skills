The Standing Collection

Objects built to outlast the hand that made them.

No object leaves the bench with a projected lifespan. It leaves when we'd hand it to our own children without a second thought.

Object 03

The Fold No. 3

A single hinge, cut from one billet instead of assembled from three parts. Fewer seams means fewer places for forty years of use to find a weakness.

Materials

Cold-forged brass-free alloy and full-grain leather, sourced from tanneries we've visited in person, not certified on paper alone.

Provenance

Every piece is stamped with a batch number tied to the week it was finished — not for marketing, so we can trace a fault back to a specific afternoon.

Guarantee

We repair anything we've made, for as long as we exist, at cost. That's the whole policy — no tiers, no fine print.

Ferrous & Fold — small batches, no seasons, made to be repaired rather than replaced.
