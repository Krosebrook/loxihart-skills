# Engagement PRD Template

Full initiative proposal. Loaded when the deliverable is a PRD. Longer than the
1-pager but still one primary reader and one core ask.

```markdown
# PRD: {Initiative name}
**Reader / sponsor:** {name, title}   **Ask:** {decision}   **Date:** {date}
**WSJF:** {score}  (BV {n} + TC {n} + RR {n}) / Job {n}

## 1. Problem & Benchmark Zero
{Current state, who's affected, status-quo cost. Figures [SOURCED]/[GENERATED].}

## 2. Goal & Measurable Outcome
{The metric. Baseline → target. Measurement window. One concrete number.}

## 3. Scope
{In scope / out of scope. What this engagement deliberately does NOT cover.}

## 4. Constraints
{Data access, compliance, budget, timeline, tooling — from discovery Q5.}

## 5. Phase Plan
{Table: Phase | Goal | Entry gate | Kill criterion | Duration.
Every gated phase has a written kill criterion.}

## 6. Risks
{Top risks + mitigation. Data, change-management, key-person, compliance.}

## 7. The Ask
{The single decision and what unblocks once it's made.}

---
Open items: {[FILL: …] list}
```

## Rules

- Benchmark Zero (§1) and Measurable Outcome (§2) are mandatory — no proposal without them.
- Every phase in §5 carries a kill criterion.
- Mark every gap `[FILL: …]`; do not fabricate scope, cost, or timeline.
