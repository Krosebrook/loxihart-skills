# Conversation Patterns — Coaching Colleagues on AI

## Universal opener

**Ask before explaining.** "What have you tried so far?" — surfaces the real
blocker faster than any tutorial and respects what they already did.

## By persona

**Curious beginner** — wants a first win, fears looking slow.
- Pattern: one real task from *their* queue, done together in 10 minutes,
  they drive the keyboard.
- Say: "Bring me the most annoying email you owe someone. We'll draft it together."
- Avoid: capability tours. Nobody adopts a tool from a demo of 40 features.

**Skeptic** — leads with objections; the objection is information.
- "It's going to take my job" → "It didn't take mine — it moved me off
  password resets onto the work that gets noticed. It types; you decide."
- "It makes things up" → agree, then aim it: "Right — which is why we use it
  for drafts and lookups where *you* check the output, not final answers."
- "No time to learn it" → "That's the point — pick the task that eats your
  time and make that the learning."
- Never argue a skeptic into enthusiasm. One useful result outworks any pitch;
  leave the door open and log the signal.

**Power user** — past the basics, hitting quality or consistency walls.
- Pattern: review an actual prompt/output pair together; introduce exactly one
  upgrade (structure, examples, or iteration), not a masterclass.
- Watch for: sensitive data creeping into prompts — coach the sanitize
  habit *now* ([COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK] posture; no client PII, no credentials).

## Closing every session

Confirm their next solo rep ("what will you run it on this week?"), then log
the adoption signal — see `references/adoption-signals.md`.
