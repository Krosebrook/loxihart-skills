# Script Structure — HeyGen Avatar Scripts

## The arc (all durations)

1. **Hook (≤10s):** the problem or the outcome, stated plainly. Never a greeting,
   never "in this video." First sentence earns the second or nothing does.
2. **Context (1–2 sentences):** why Kyle has standing to say this — the
   anchor sentence lives here or in Proof.
3. **Body (3 beats max):** one idea per beat, concrete noun in each. Three
   beats fill 60–90s; cutting a beat beats compressing three into mush.
4. **Proof (1 beat):** the receipt — what was built/run/measured. If there's
   no receipt, the script isn't ready.
5. **CTA (1 sentence):** one action. Default per SKILL.md: "Find me at
   FlashFusion.co" unless a CTA destination was specified.

## Timing math (plan before writing)

Avatar delivery ≈ 145–155 wpm. Budget:

| Runtime | Word target | Beats |
|---|---|---|
| 30s | ~70–80 | hook + 1 + CTA |
| 60s | ~140–155 | hook + 2–3 + proof + CTA |
| 90s | ~215–230 | full arc |

Over budget → cut a beat, never talk faster.

## Paired output (every script ships with both blocks)

**Script Block** — the words, with `[beat]` markers only where a pause does work.
**Production Prompt Block** — Look selection, Voice Director tags (pace/energy/
emphasis lines), B-roll suggestions per beat, on-screen text moments, and the
credit estimate. Structure per `references/production-prompt.md`.

## QA before handoff

Read aloud once (ear test), banned-list scan (voice-rules), timing recount,
CTA singular, at least one anchor sentence present.
