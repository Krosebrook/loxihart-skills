# ROI Frameworks by AI Use Case

## Document Processing (Claims, Invoices, Applications)
| Metric | Benchmark Range | Source |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| Labor reduction | 60-80% of processing time | Industry average for AI document extraction |
| Error reduction | 50-90% fewer errors | Depends on document complexity |
| Cycle time reduction | 70-90% faster | From hours to minutes |
| Typical payback | 6-12 months | For 1000+ documents/month |

## Customer Support (Chatbots, Ticket Routing, Agent Assist)
| Metric | Benchmark Range | Source |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| Ticket deflection | 20-40% handled by AI | First-line resolution without human |
| Agent handle time reduction | 20-35% shorter | AI-suggested responses, auto-categorization |
| Customer satisfaction | +5-15 NPS points | Faster resolution, 24/7 availability |
| Typical payback | 3-9 months | For 500+ tickets/month |

## Content Generation (Marketing, Reports, Documentation)
| Metric | Benchmark Range | Source |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| Content creation speed | 3-5x faster | Draft generation, not final output |
| Human editing time | 30-50% of total | AI draft + human refinement |
| Volume increase | 2-4x more content | Same team, more output |
| Typical payback | 3-6 months | For content teams of 3+ |

## Data Analysis (Business Intelligence, Anomaly Detection)
| Metric | Benchmark Range | Source |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| Analysis time reduction | 50-70% faster | Automated pattern detection |
| Insight discovery | 2-3x more insights | AI finds patterns humans miss |
| Decision speed | 40-60% faster | Real-time vs. weekly reports |
| Typical payback | 6-18 months | Depends on data maturity |

## Common Pitfalls in ROI Models

1. **Overclaiming accuracy** — Don't promise 99% accuracy. Use 85-95% and show the human review layer.
2. **Ignoring change management** — Budget 10-20% for training, adoption, and process redesign.
3. **Forgetting maintenance** — AI systems need ongoing tuning. Budget 15-25% of implementation for annual maintenance.
4. **Using peak performance as baseline** — Use average performance, not the best-case demo.
5. **Ignoring opportunity cost of doing nothing** — Model what happens if the client doesn't invest (competitor advantage, talent retention).
6. **Single-scenario presentation** — Always show conservative, expected, and optimistic. Executives distrust single-number projections.
