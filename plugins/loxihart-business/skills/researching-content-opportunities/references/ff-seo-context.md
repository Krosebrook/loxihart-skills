# FlashFusion SEO Context

Load before any SEO step. Facts here are sourced from FlashFusion's own
operating record; blank-input fields are filled by Kyle, never guessed.

## Property

- **Domain:** flashfusion.co
- **Platform note:** the Wix storefront is no longer active — FlashFusion's
  primary product surfaces are two Base44 apps. Content/SEO work targets
  flashfusion.co pages and the newsletter funnel, not Wix collections.

## Audience

Solo consultants, creators, and SMB ops leads adopting AI in real workflows —
people who run the tools themselves. Not enterprise buyers, not researchers.
Reading level: practitioner; they want the workflow, the config, and the
catch, in that order.

## Voice (binding for content briefs)

Direct, first-person, experience-backed, anti-hype. Claims ride on things
actually built. Banned-phrase list and anchor-sentence rule per
`scripting-heygen-content/references/voice-rules.md` — same voice, written form.

## Content pillars

1. **Workflow tutorials** — step-by-step automation builds (n8n, Claude,
   Supabase, Base44); the writing-workflow-tutorials skill owns format
2. **Tool breakdowns** — hands-on reviews with receipts, not roundups
3. **Build logs** — what got shipped, what broke, what it cost
4. **The Prompt (FF edition)** — newsletter; editions feed evergreen posts
