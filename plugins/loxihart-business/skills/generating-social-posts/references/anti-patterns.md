# Anti-Patterns — What Kyle's Voice Is NOT

Use this file as a rejection filter. If a draft matches any pattern below, rewrite before delivering.

---

## Tone anti-patterns

### The LinkedIn Influencer Move
Performs insight instead of stating it. Editorializes the lesson rather than demonstrating it.

❌ "I had a major realization that changed the way I think about AI forever."
✅ "I stopped prompting for outputs. I started designing flows."

❌ "This one simple trick saved me hours every week."
✅ "Keep active code off cloud-synced folders. Boring. Saves hours a week."

---

### Performed Vulnerability
Names the emotion instead of describing the state technically.

❌ "It was a really hard time in my life and I struggled a lot."
✅ "The cognitive architecture I was born with stopped working the way it used to."

❌ "I was scared and didn't know what to do."
✅ "Recovery assumes there's something to go back to. That wasn't the situation."

---

### The Humble Brag Setup
Uses false modesty to front-load credentials.

❌ "I'm just a self-taught builder, but I've shipped 20+ AI apps and worked with Fortune 500 companies..."
✅ "I'm an L1 service desk tech who builds AI apps on the side. This list is what I check before I ship."

---

### Generic Advice That Applies to 1,000 People
No tool name. No failure mode. No specific number. Could have been written by anyone.

❌ "AI is a powerful tool, but you need to use it strategically to see real results."
✅ "If you treat AI like a magic API call, you'll ship something that works once. If you treat it like a system of unreliable junior operators, you'll build something that holds up."

---

### Advice-Giving Tone
Talks at the reader like a coach instead of alongside them like a builder who just found out.

❌ "Here are 5 things you should do to improve your AI workflows."
✅ "I split it into: spec generator → validator → codegen → test pass. Each step checks the last. Failures dropped. Output quality jumped."

---

### The Question Hook
Opening with a rhetorical question signals a content creator, not a builder.

❌ "Have you ever wondered why your AI apps keep failing in production?"
✅ "Most AI apps fail in production for the same reason. It's not the model."

❌ "What if I told you there's a better way to handle secrets in your Next.js app?"
✅ "Grep your production bundle before you ship."

---

### Cliché phrases (hard ban)
- "in today's world" / "in today's fast-paced environment"
- "leverage AI" / "harness the power of"
- "game-changer" / "paradigm shift"
- "at the end of the day"
- "and that's when I realized"
- "I hope this helps someone"
- "food for thought"
- "it goes without saying"
- "let's dive in"
- "unpacking" (as a verb for explaining something)

---

### The Soft Closer
Ends with a generic invitation or a question that dissipates momentum.

❌ "What do you think? Let me know in the comments!"
❌ "I'd love to hear your thoughts on this."
✅ "The boring habits compound. The clever ones don't."
✅ "The plus is the entire job."
✅ "It's a comment, not authentication."

---

### Inflated Credentials
Any claim about tool count, client roster, or impact that isn't grounded in a specific, verifiable thing.

❌ "With 200+ AI tools under my belt and dozens of enterprise deployments..."
✅ "I've used about 30 tools with real hands-on depth. Here's what I've actually seen break."

---

### Overly Literary for the Platform
Blog can go long and literary. LinkedIn can't. Twitter/X can't. Calibrate register to platform.

❌ (on LinkedIn) "In the quiet hours after my daughter falls asleep, I find myself returning to the question of what it means to build in public..."
✅ (on LinkedIn) "I write most of this at night, after my daughter's asleep. The question I keep coming back to: what does it cost to build in public?"

---

## Audio script anti-patterns

❌ Bullet points read aloud: "Number one... number two... number three..."
✅ Spoken transitions: "First thing. Then. Here's the part most people skip."

❌ Markdown in the script: "Check out the link **here**"
✅ Verbal reference: "Link's in the show notes."

❌ Sentences that end with qualifiers: "...and that's kind of the main thing, I guess."
✅ Sentences that end decisively: "That's the whole move."
