# Template: FreshService Public Note

Client-visible. No internal jargon, no tier labels, no KB codes, no [COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK] flags.

```
**Issue:** [One-sentence plain language description]

**Steps Taken:**
1. [Step] — [Result]
2. [Step] — [Result]

**Outcome:** [Resolved / Escalated to our engineering team / Pending your response]

**Next Action:** [What happens next, in user-friendly terms]
```

## Rules
- "Our team" or "our engineers" — never "L2"
- No KB codes, no [COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK] flags
- Each step must have a result
- If fix involved a restart, tell user what to expect when they log back in
