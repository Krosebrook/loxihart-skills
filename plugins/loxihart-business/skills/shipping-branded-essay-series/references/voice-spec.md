# Voice Spec

Canonical sources: `03-style.md` and `voice-audit.jsx` in the project. Read them
directly before drafting — this file is the working summary, they are ground truth.

## Moves

- **Open by reading the room.** Start with the problem the reader already feels —
  never the thesis, never an announcement. No opener that begins with a question.
- **Sentence length is punctuation.** Short. Then a longer one that earns it.
  Short to close. If a paragraph runs at one pace throughout, it's broken.
- **Technical precision carries the emotional weight.** Describe feelings with the
  specificity of a bug report. Find the system metaphor; remove the feeling word;
  let the metaphor carry it.
- **Acknowledge what he isn't before what he is.** The anti-credential move is the
  credibility move.
- **Heavy things get dropped quietly and moved past.** One phrase, then keep
  walking. Never a paragraph of reflection.
- **Same skill, different substrate.** Show the diagnostic-instinct transfer
  (bar → QC → systems); don't name it. Put both contexts in one sentence and let
  the parallel do the work.
- **End on an observation or declarative.** Never a summary. Never warm. The last
  sentence should make the reader sit, not nod.
- **The lanes bleed** (build + life are one story) — but only within allowed
  lanes. Never into the day job or the memoir's protected material.

## Banned — instant rewrite

leverage · game-changer · paradigm shift · harness the power of · in today's
world · unlock your potential · delve · dive in · "here's what actually happened"
· "looking back" · "and that's when I realized" · "rock bottom became..." ·
"grateful for the journey" · any sentence that could run unchanged in a self-help
book · any construction a LinkedIn influencer would post unironically.

## Structural bans

- No opener that is a question.
- No closer that summarizes the argument.
- No editorial tells ("Here's what actually happened:", "Looking back...").
- No emoji, no hashtags in essay copy.

## Closer order (when useful)

Paradox → reframe → the plus move → declarative. Land it short.

## FAIL conditions for a drafted post

- Opens with a question or an announcement of the thesis.
- Closes with a summary or a warm takeaway.
- Contains any banned word/phrase.
- First sentence could apply to any AI newsletter unchanged.
- Describes an emotion with an adjective where a system metaphor was available.
