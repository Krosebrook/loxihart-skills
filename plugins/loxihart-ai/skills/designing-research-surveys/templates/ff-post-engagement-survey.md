# Template — Post-Engagement Client Feedback

**Use:** within 48 hours of final delivery — response rates fall off a cliff
after a week. SurveyMonkey link in the wrap-up email, 4–6 questions visible
in the email body as a preview. ~2 minutes, 7 questions.

## Questions

1. Overall, how satisfied are you with this engagement? 1–5
2. Did the engagement deliver the outcome we scoped? ○ Yes ○ Partly ○ No
   → If Partly/No: what's the gap? *(open text)*
3. What was the single most valuable part? *(open text)*
4. What should we do differently next time? *(open text)*
5. How likely are you to recommend FlashFusion to a peer? 0–10
6. Anything you'd want help with next? *(open text — pipeline signal, not a pitch)*
7. May we quote your feedback?
   ○ Yes, with name/company ○ Yes, anonymized ○ No
   → If yes: the sentence you'd stand behind: *(open text)*

## Analysis keys

- Q1 + Q5 go on the engagement record (HubSpot) as-is.
- Q3/Q7 quotes → testimonial/case-study raw material — **only** with the Q7
  consent level actually given, verbatim, no touch-ups.
- Q4 answers get one written follow-up ("here's what we changed") — the
  survey is a loop, not a mailbox.
- Q6 responses route to pipeline as a warm signal with context attached.
- N will be small. Report answers as what this client said — never percentages
  over a handful of engagements.
